1.  Billable Utilization Rate
Target/Benchmark: 65-72%
Formula: (Billable Hours ÷ Total Hours) × 100
Rationale: Lower due to mentorship and leadership responsibilities.
Success Criteria: 68-70% average; 10% for mentoring.
2.  Project Deadline Adherence
Target/Benchmark: ≥90%
Formula: (Milestones Met On-Time ÷ Total Milestones) × 100
Rationale: Ensures timely delivery.
Success Criteria: 90%+ milestones on-time.
3.  Quality & Error Reduction

Rationale: Maintains minimal errors through QA/QC adherence.
Success Criteria: Minimal errors per project.
4.  Mentorship and Team Development
Target/Benchmark: 4+ sessions/year
Rationale: Builds firm capability through knowledge transfer.
Success Criteria: Delivering at least 4 sessions yearly.
5.  Studio Citizenship Score
Target/Benchmark: ≥3.50
Formula: (Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)
Rationale: Demonstrates exemplary citizenship and cultural stewardship.
Success Criteria: Solid (3.50-3.99), Outstanding (4.00-4.49), Exceptional (4.50-5.00)
