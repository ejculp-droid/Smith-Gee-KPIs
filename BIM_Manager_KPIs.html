1.  Utilization Rate
Target/Benchmark: 55-65%
Formula: (Direct Labor Hours ÷ Total Labor Hours) × 100
Rationale: Reflects balance with training, standards development, and management.
Success Criteria: 58-62% average.
2.  Model Delivery On-Time
Target/Benchmark: 85-95%
Formula: (BIM Deliverables On Schedule ÷ Total BIM Deliverables) × 100
Rationale: Ensures BIM coordination aligns with project timelines.
Success Criteria: 90%+ delivered on schedule.

3.  BIM Standards Compliance
Target/Benchmark: 96-100%
Formula: (Projects Meeting BIM Standards ÷ Total Projects) × 100
Rationale: Enforces BIM standards across projects.
Success Criteria: 98%+ compliance.
4.  Clash Detection Pre-Issue
Target/Benchmark: 85-95%
Formula: (Clashes Resolved Before Issue ÷ Total Clashes Found) × 100
Rationale: Prevents construction issues by catching problems early.
Success Criteria: 90%+ resolved before issue.
5.  Team BIM Training Hours
Target/Benchmark: 40+ hrs/year
Rationale: Develops team BIM skills.
Success Criteria: Delivering 40+ hours annually.
6.  Studio Citizenship Score
Target/Benchmark: ≥3.50
Formula: (Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)
Rationale: Reflects cross-discipline collaboration and cultural leadership.
Success Criteria: Solid (3.50-3.99), Outstanding (4.00-4.49), Exceptional (4.50-5.00).
