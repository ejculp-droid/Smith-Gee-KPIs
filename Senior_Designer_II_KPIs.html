1.  Billable Utilization Rate
Target/Benchmark: 60-68%
Formula: (Billable Hours ÷ Total Hours) × 100
Rationale: Reflects substantial leadership responsibilities.
Success Criteria: 63-65% average; 15-20% for leadership.
2.  On-Time, Complete & Correct Delivery (OTCC)
Target/Benchmark: ≥95%
Formula: (Milestones Delivered OTCC ÷ Total Milestones) × 100
Rationale: Ensures high standards for timely and accurate work.
Success Criteria: 95%+ OTCC delivery.
3.  Project Budget Adherence
Target/Benchmark: <5% overrun
Rationale: Controls costs on phases led.
Success Criteria: Limit overruns to <5%.

4.  Project Profitability
Target/Benchmark: ≥15%
Formula: ((Revenue - Costs) ÷ Revenue) × 100
Rationale: Ensures efficiency and scope control.
Success Criteria: 15%+ average profit margin.
5.  Studio Citizenship Score
Target/Benchmark: ≥3.50
Formula: (Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)
Rationale: Demonstrates exceptional citizenship as a cultural leader.
Success Criteria: Solid (3.50-3.99), Outstanding (4.00-4.49), Exceptional (4.50-5.00)
