1.  Billable Utilization Rate
Target/Benchmark: 70-80%
Formula: (Billable Hours ÷ Total Hours) × 100
Rationale: Maximizes productive time while learning.
Success Criteria: 75% average; 15% for training.
2.  Task Completion Timeliness
Target/Benchmark: 88-94%
Formula: (Tasks Completed On-Time ÷ Total Tasks) × 100
Rationale: Demonstrates reliability and time management.
Success Criteria: 90%+ on-time.
3.  Redline Response Accuracy
Target/Benchmark: ≥95%, <2 rounds
Rationale: Minimizes rework through attention to detail.
Success Criteria: Accurate incorporations with <2 correction rounds.
4.  BIM/CAD Standards Compliance
Target/Benchmark: 92-96%
Formula: (Compliant Deliverables ÷ Total Deliverables) × 100
Rationale: Maintains quality through adherence to standards.
Success Criteria: 94%+ compliance.
5.  Learning Progression
Target/Benchmark: 1 per quarter
Rationale: Encourages continuous skill development.
Success Criteria: Completing at least one module per quarter.
6.  Mentor Check-Ins & Feedback Engagement

Target/Benchmark: Monthly
Rationale: Active engagement accelerates development.
Success Criteria: Participation in monthly sessions.
7.  Studio Citizenship Score
Target/Benchmark: ≥3.50
Formula: (Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)
Rationale: Demonstrates strong foundation in studio citizenship.
Success Criteria: Solid (3.50-3.99), Outstanding (4.00-4.49), Exceptional (4.50-5.00)
