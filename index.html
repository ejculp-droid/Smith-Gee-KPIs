Senior Project Manager KPIs

  • Team Utilization Rate: 68-72%
    (Total Team Direct Labor Hours ÷ Total Team Hours) × 100
  • Team Portfolio Profitability: 20-24%
    Sum of [(Revenue - Costs) ÷ Revenue] ÷ Number of Projects × 100
  • Direct Project Profitability: 18-22%
    (Your Project Revenue - Your Project Costs) ÷ Your Project Revenue × 100
  • Portfolio Budget Variance: Positive Variance
    Sum of (Budgeted Cost - Actual Cost) across all projects
  • Team On-Time Delivery Rate: 80%+
    (Team Projects Delivered On-Time ÷ Total Team Projects) × 100
  • Team Average Realization Rate: 94%+
    Average [(Actual Revenue ÷ (Hours × Standard Rates)) × 100] across projects
  • Portfolio Change Order Profitability: 65-75%
    (Change Order Revenue - Change Order Costs) ÷ Change Order Revenue × 100
  • Studio Citizenship Score: ≥3.50
    (Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)

Studio Citizenship Score

This score is calculated as a weighted sum:

(Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)

  • Supervisor Score: Based on leadership, initiative, contribution to the studio culture
  • Peer Score: Based on collaboration, teamwork, and support
  • Target Score: 3.50 or higher on a 5-point scale
