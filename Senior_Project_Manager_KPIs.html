1.  Team Utilization Rate
Target/Benchmark: 68-72%
Formula: (Total Team Direct Labor Hours ÷ Total Team Hours) × 100
Rationale: Measures the effective allocation of team time to revenue-generating 
project work.
Success Criteria: Achieving a 70% average with all Project Managers maintaining rates 
above 65%.
2.  Team Portfolio Profitability
Target/Benchmark: 20-24%
Formula: Sum of [(Revenue - Costs) ÷ Revenue] ÷ Number of Projects × 100
Rationale: Reflects financial performance and oversight in resource allocation and 
cost control.
Success Criteria: Achieving 22% or higher with no individual project below 15% 
without approval.
3.  Direct Project Profitability
Target/Benchmark: 18-22%
Formula: (Your Project Revenue - Your Project Costs) ÷ Your Project Revenue × 100
Rationale: Evaluates individual project management skills.
Success Criteria: Achieving a 20%+ margin on direct projects.
4.  Portfolio Budget Variance
Target/Benchmark: Positive Variance
Rationale: Demonstrates the ability to oversee multiple projects and maintain 
financial control.
Success Criteria: A $100,000 or greater surplus annually.
5.  Team On-Time Delivery Rate
Target/Benchmark: 80%+
Formula: (Team Projects Delivered On-Time ÷ Total Team Projects) × 100
Rationale: Indicates the ability to meet schedule commitments affecting client 
satisfaction.
Success Criteria: An on-time delivery rate of 80% or higher.
6.  Team Average Realization Rate
Target/Benchmark: 94%+
Formula: Average [(Actual Revenue ÷ (Hours × Standard Rates)) × 100] across projects
Rationale: Measures billing efficiency and scope management.
Success Criteria: Maintaining an average realization rate of 94% or higher.
7.  Portfolio Change Order Profitability
Target/Benchmark: 65-75%
Formula: (Change Order Revenue - Change Order Costs) ÷ Change Order Revenue × 
100
Rationale: Reflects the ability to price changes effectively and control associated 
costs.
Success Criteria: Consistently achieving a 70%+ margin.
8.  Studio Citizenship Score
Target/Benchmark: ≥3.50
Formula: (Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)
Rationale: Reflects leadership in fostering a positive culture.

Success Criteria: Solid (3.50-3.99), Outstanding (4.00-4.49), Exceptional (4.50-5.00)
