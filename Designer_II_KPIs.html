1.  Billable Utilization Rate
Target/Benchmark: 72-82%
Formula: (Billable Hours ÷ Total Hours) × 100
Rationale: Reflects increased productivity.
Success Criteria: 77% average.
2.  On-Time Task Completion
Target/Benchmark: 90-96%
Formula: (Tasks Delivered On-Time ÷ Total Tasks) × 100
Rationale: Ensures reliability on complex tasks.
Success Criteria: 93%+ on-time.
3.  QA/QC Compliance
Target/Benchmark: 100%
Formula: (Deliverables QA/QC'd ÷ Total Deliverables) × 100
Rationale: Quality control discipline.

Success Criteria: 100% reviewed and resolved.
4.  Drawing Accuracy
Target/Benchmark: <5% rework
Rationale: Produces high-quality output.
Success Criteria: Less than 5% rework required.
5.  Professional Development
Target/Benchmark: ≥20 hrs/year
Rationale: Continued learning for advancement.
Success Criteria: Completing 20+ hours annually.
6.  Studio Citizenship Score
Target/Benchmark: ≥3.50
Formula: (Supervisor Score × 0.75) + (Peer Score × 0.25)
Rationale: Expected to contribute actively to team support.
Success Criteria: Solid (3.50-3.99), Outstanding (4.00-4.49), Exceptional (4.50-5.00)
